✨ A LITTLE SURPRISE FOR YOU ✨

Sangita 🌙❤️

Kabhi-kabhi kisi ke liye
kuch special karne ke liye
duniya ki saari cheezein nahi chahiye...

Bas ek sachhi feeling aur dil se nikli kuch baatein
kaafi hoti hain. ❤️

Tumne kaha tha...
“Mujhe Chand-Taare laakar doge?” 🌙✨

Chand toh aasman mein hi rehne do...
kyunki agar main use tod kar le bhi aaun,
toh tumhari aankhon ki chamak ke
saamne woh bhi kam lagega. 🥹❤️
Sangita... ❤️

Shayad main tumhe
har woh cheez na de paun
jo tum deserve karti ho...

Lekin ek cheez hamesha de sakta hoon—
dil se nikli hui respect, care aur ek genuine smile. ❤️

Aur haan...
tumne Chand-Taare maange the na? 🌙✨

Chand aur Taare toh bahut log dikha denge...
main bas itna chahta hoon
ki tumhari life mein meri wajah se
thodi si khushi zaroor rahe. 🫶

— Chiku ❤️
